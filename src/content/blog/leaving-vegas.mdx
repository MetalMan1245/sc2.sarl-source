What's going on in life?

I've been living in Las Vegas for about a year, and in less than a month I'll be moving away.  Honestly, I won't really miss it.  I've met people in Vegas that I will miss, and that's what it's about for me.  I don't necessarily care where I live nearly as much as who I live near.  But at the end of the day I moved to Las Vegas for opportunities in the audio industry, I found an opportunity giving tours at Allegiant Stadium for the Raiders, and as far as corporate jobs go it's actually been quite good.  But I really found audio work back in Kingman, at the Beale Street Theater, and that's where I intend to go.

We're still looking for a place to live, but if anybody in Kingman is interested, stay away from Copper Ridge apartments, they lied about their pricing on the webaite, confirmed the incorrect pricing to me over the phone, and misrepresented how a stated promotion worked, to get me to come in, verify my identity online (the property manager never even checked that our identities were verified she just made us do it for tbe data mining) and look at an apartment that was now north of $300 a month more than advertised in the best of cases, $500 under normal circumstances.  I am in the process of filing a complaint with the Better Business Bureau, I don't like it when people lie and get away with it.  And even if it was a series of genuine mistakes, that's far too many mistakes and the property manager showed 0 remorse, which to me said she either didn't care or knew to begin with.

What Have I been Working On?

Despite my best efforts to stay focused on packing I've been churning away at several projects, mostly related to local AI and self hosting.  If you're sick of everything being a subscription, requiring an account, and generally being a privacy violation nightmare, I recommend looking into self hosting, you can basically replicate all cloud services yourself if you want to put the time into it.
